# Contributing

New tasks require:

1. A unique immutable ID and revision.
2. User-visible target disambiguation.
3. Explicit success predicates and protected invariants.
4. An allowlist of permitted mutations.
5. Deterministic setup/reset and evaluator tests.
6. A human reference trajectory and step budget.
7. Fixture ownership/license and publication permission.
8. At least three repeated validation runs.
9. Safety and privacy review.

Do not commit raw browser profiles, authenticated traces, or unlicensed media.
